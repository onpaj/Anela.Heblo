Scenario: invoice A is new — `GetOrCreateAsync` (line 139) calls `AddAsync`, tracking it as `Added`, but does not save. If anything between line 90 (`_mapper.Map(invoiceDetail, invoice)`) and line 113 throws *without* being caught by the inner ERP-sync try/catch (lines 99-113) — e.g. an AutoMapper exception on the refresh-map call, or an exception from `transformation.TransformAsync` at line 96 — the outer catch (line 126) logs and rethrows, `ImportInvoicesAsync`'s per-invoice catch (line 61) records invoice A as `Failed` and moves on, and `_repository.SaveChangesAsync` is **never called for invoice A**. However, invoice A's entity remains tracked as `Added` in the shared `DbContext`'s change tracker — nothing detaches or removes it. When invoice B (next in the same batch, or in a later batch) is imported successfully and reaches `await _repository.SaveChangesAsync(cancellationToken)` at line 122, EF Core flushes the *entire* tracked graph, not just invoice B — silently INSERTing invoice A's incomplete row (mapped only via the factory, never refreshed, never transformed, with no sync outcome recorded) even though invoice A was reported as `Failed` and its own flow never completed. This directly violates FR-3's acceptance criterion ("No new invoice row exists in the database unless the entire `ExecuteImportInvoice` flow ... has completed and the single `SaveChangesAsync` has succeeded") and is a regression versus the pre-fix code, where `AddAsync` + `SaveChangesAsync` were unconditionally back-to-back inside `GetOrCreateAsync` with no possibility of an intervening exception, so a new invoice was never left dangling in the tracker across iterations. Fix needs to ensure a failed invoice's entity is detached/discarded from the change tracker (or otherwise never left in `Added` state) before the loop moves to the next invoice — e.g. detach `invoice` in the outer catch when `isNew` is true, or defer the `AddAsync` call itself until immediately before the final `SaveChangesAsync` so a thrown exception before that point never leaves an orphaned tracked entity.
