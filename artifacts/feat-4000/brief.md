`frontend/src/pages/ExpeditionListArchivePage.tsx` bundles four distinct responsibilities into one component:

The component resolves 6+ hooks, 5 different mutation handlers, and 3 independent permission checks, making it hard to test any single concern in isolation.

## Why it matters

- **Single Responsibility violation**: A change to job-control behaviour, or to the print-fix workflow, forces edits to the archive page and vice versa.
- **Hidden cross-module coupling**: Frontend module boundaries are less formal than backend ones, but the `ExpeditionListArchive` page importing directly from `useExpeditionList` means changes to that hook can silently break this page.
- **Test burden**: Mocking the full dependency graph (blob API, recurring jobs API, print-fix API, print-order API) to test date-selection behaviour taxes every future test author.

## Suggested fix

Extract the operational controls into a co-located sub-component (e.g. `ExpeditionJobControlsBar`) that receives the relevant hooks and handlers as props, leaving `ExpeditionListArchivePage` responsible only for the archive browsing (date sidebar + items table + reprint dialog).

The `PrintOrderModal` and the print-fix button could live in `ExpeditionJobControlsBar`, or the page could use a thin container that composes `ExpeditionJobControlsBar` and the archive body. The archive browsing tests would then only mock the three archive-related hooks.
