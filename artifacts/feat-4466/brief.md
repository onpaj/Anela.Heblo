## Why it matters
- **Two sources of truth** for business rules: the backend enforces the real state machine; the frontend applies its own copy to decide which UI controls to render. When they diverge, the UI can show incorrect available actions — e.g. a "Confirm Completion" button on an order the backend will reject.
- **Violation of SRP**: a display component (`ManufactureOrderDetail`) is deciding business state-machine policy, not just rendering it.
